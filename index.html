Daily % needed to reach a target balance over a set number of weeks.

Compound: (target ÷ start)^(1 ÷ trading days) − 1. Day 1 and Last day show the dollar gain needed on your first and final trading day. Assumes every trading day is a winner and ignores fees and taxes. Green is under 1% a day, amber 1–2.5%, red above 2.5%.

Day 1 and Last day show the dollars needed that day, with ticks per contract underneath (dollars ÷ contracts ÷ tick value). Tick values are standard CME contract specs; confirm on cmegroup.com before trading, since specs can change.
